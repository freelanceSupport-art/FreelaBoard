# Social Posts

## Short Launch Post

FreelaBoard is a local Windows app for freelancers who want one place to manage projects, deadlines, invoice status, payment status, and CSV exports.

No account. No cloud dependency. Just a single EXE and local SQLite storage.

## Feature Post

FreelaBoard now supports Windows notification-area deadline alerts. Close the window, keep the app resident, and reopen or exit from the tray icon menu.

## Problem/Solution Post

Freelance project tracking often gets split across notes, spreadsheets, and calendar reminders. FreelaBoard puts project status, due dates, invoices, and payments into one local desktop cockpit.

## Release Caption

FreelaBoard v0.2.0: local freelance project tracking with deadline alerts, resident tray mode, invoice/payment states, and CSV export.
